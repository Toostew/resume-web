About me

I'm a first-year Computer Science student at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS, drawn to the invisible side of tech: the firmware that drives hardware, the infrastructure that keeps systems alive and the backend that makes everything else possible. On the software side, I build backend systems in Java and Spring Boot, deploying them on self-managed VPS and homelab environments because I'd rather own the stack than sell it away to abstraction. On the hardware side, I'm currently going bare-metal on an STM32 NUCLEO-G474RE, writing drivers, exploring RTOS internals, and understanding what actually happens below the HAL. I'm interested in embedded and backend systems, firmware, and infrastructure engineering, building the low-level reliability that high-level tech is built upon.
