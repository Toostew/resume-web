On the topic of AI

A note on how I use it, and where I stand.

Personally, I believe like many things, AI is a tool. A force multiplier. It is an absolute god-send for most developers, particularly those who already have experience; It's basically the perfect junior engineer that does everything you ask of it. But for people like me, the learners, the ones just getting into the field, I need to be mindful and strategic with how I use AI. I say this because I firmly believe if used incorrectly AI actually stunts growth and learning, and just creates compliant engineers who cannot function on their own. Which is why I have a very specific use-case for AI. For me, I use it almost exclusively as a guide, to answer specific questions on design philosophies, choices, implementation details, architecture details, etc. I treat AI like an always-on teacher I can use as reference, to accelerate my own learning. But beyond that, I try to limit my use and dependence in order to maintain my learning. I prioritize implementing, reasoning and making choices myself. However, an exception to this is frontend, since I have no interest in learning the syntax or design patterns for it, I delegate this to AI to design and implement it up to my spec.

TLDR: What I use, and how

Claude, Gemini (Web) Frontend work, General guidance, Random questions
Never for Direct implementation of core logic, backend or anything other than frontend work

AI is powerful. There's no doubt about that. But if I use it wrong I'll grow to only BELIEVE I'm capable, but not actually be. I fear I'll only be as good as the model I'm using; and that terrifies me.
